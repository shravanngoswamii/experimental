Experimental workspace guide

A minimal map of the main folders in this repository for quick navigation.

Key folders

Path Purpose Notes
DoodlePPL/ Active DoodleBUGS and multi-canvas experiments. Includes Vue/Vite apps plus Julia runtime components.
GSoC-Backup/ Archived snapshots of earlier implementations. Useful for diffing behavior and recovering old ideas.
sample/ Astro sample project for quick experiments. Contains pages, layouts, styles, and static assets.
JOSS-reviews/ Paper and review support material. Includes references and markdown-to-pdf helpers.
Research-Work/ Drafts of ongoing research writing. Multiple project-specific paper draft folders.
tools/ and navbar/ Small automation scripts. Project discovery and navbar insertion utilities.
